1. Who we are

India Explorer is developed and published by Nishaan Tech ("Nishaan Tech", "we", "us" or "our"), an independent app studio based in India. This policy applies to the India Explorer Android application and its associated app experience.

App name: India Explorer
Android package: com.nishaantech.exploreindia

2. Who the app is for

India Explorer is designed as a child-friendly educational app for children and families. During onboarding, a user can choose one of these age bands to tailor the learning experience: 3–5, 6–7, 8–9 or 10–12.

A parent or legal guardian should help a child install and use the app and review this policy with them. Please do not enter a child's full legal name or other sensitive information into the app.

This policy reflects the public develop branch of the app repository reviewed on 9 November 2026. Production configuration, build-time settings, or later code changes may affect actual data flows. We review the policy when those practices change.

3. Information stored on your device

The current app stores its player profile, learning progress and preferences in local storage within the app's WebView. Based on the reviewed code, the app does not require a user account or sign-in to use these features.

These records are intended to remain in the app's local storage and are not automatically uploaded as a player profile. The Android manifest reviewed for this version sets android:allowBackup="false". This disables Android's standard app backup behavior for the app, but should not be interpreted as a guarantee about every device-level transfer, screenshot, or user-initiated export mechanism.

Names and ages: In the current onboarding route, the displayed explorer name is derived from the selected avatar, and the selected age band is saved locally. The age band is not the child's date of birth.

4. Online services and diagnostics

The app's source repository includes Firebase libraries and configuration code. We reviewed how those libraries are used in the current develop branch:

  • The Firebase Analytics event wrapper currently exits before logging an event. The app lifecycle code also explicitly keeps Analytics disabled.
  • The repository contains a Firestore error-reporting implementation. It can attempt to upload diagnostic details if a Firebase app is initialized and an error is passed to that reporting function.
  • We did not find a call to the Firebase initialization function elsewhere in the reviewed app source. Therefore, we did not verify an active Firebase Analytics or Firestore upload path in this branch.

If Firebase error reporting is enabled in a production build or a later release, a diagnostic record may include an error message, stack details, error boundary or screen information, browser/device user-agent string, page URL, and any technical context attached to the error. Such details should not contain a child's name or other direct identifiers; error text and context should nevertheless be reviewed for accidental personal information.

The app also references Google Fonts through a stylesheet hosted by Google. When the app loads that stylesheet while online, the device makes a request to Google's font service. Google may receive normal connection information such as IP address, request headers and browser/device information as part of serving the request.

5. Postcards and sharing

Children can create postcards for states and save them to the in-app Passport. The postcard library and image data are stored locally in the app.

When a user chooses the postcard sharing feature on Android, the app writes a temporary image file to the app's cache directory and opens the Android share sheet. The user selects the receiving app or destination. The selected destination receives the item the user chooses to share and handles it under its own privacy policy and terms.

On a web build, the corresponding fallback can download the postcard image locally. Sharing and downloading are user-initiated actions; the postcard is not automatically published by Nishaan Tech. Parents and guardians should supervise sharing, especially when the receiving app is a messaging or social platform.

Important: In the current postcard screen, the Share button calls the sharing function directly. The handler explicitly notes that no PIN gate is required, even though an older UI comment labels the button "Parent-gated". The reviewed flow therefore does not enforce a parent PIN before sharing. Parents should supervise the child directly rather than relying on a parent-only sharing lock.

6. Permissions and device features

The Android manifest reviewed requests these permissions:

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): allows the app to make network requests, including loading remote resources such as the Google Fonts stylesheet and any configured online integrations. The permission itself does not mean player profile or progress data is transmitted.
  • Modify audio settings (android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): supports audio playback and audio settings.

The app also integrates the device's text-to-speech engine, app-cache file access for temporary postcard sharing, and the Android share sheet. The reviewed manifest does not declare camera, microphone, contacts, precise-location or external-storage write permissions.

Text-to-speech is used to play text aloud; the app is not designed to record a child's voice. Depending on the device and selected speech engine, the engine provider may process speech text under its own settings and privacy terms. Review the device's TTS provider settings for details.

7. Third-party services

The current dependency manifest includes Firebase packages for Analytics and Firestore, Capacitor integrations for text-to-speech, filesystem and sharing, and the app references Google's hosted font stylesheet.

  • Google Fonts: the app requests font stylesheets from Google Fonts. Google's Privacy Policy describes its handling of service request data.
  • Google Firebase: Firebase Analytics and Firestore code is present, but the current source review did not confirm an active initialized data-upload path. See Firebase privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Device text-to-speech: speech playback is provided by the TTS engine available on the device. The engine's provider may have separate privacy terms.
  • Android share targets: when you choose another app in the system share sheet, that app receives the item you choose to share and handles it under its own policy.

We did not find an advertising SDK, ad network integration, payment SDK or social-login SDK in the current package.json. This source-file check is not a substitute for inspecting the final Android application bundle for any SDKs added outside that manifest.

8. Children's privacy

India Explorer is intended for children and families. We aim to use only the information needed to provide the app's learning features. Parents and guardians should help children choose an avatar and age band, supervise postcard sharing, and contact us with any privacy concern.

The repository still contains an older parental-consent screen component, but the active onboarding route reviewed currently asks the user to select an avatar and age band and then saves the profile. The current route does not include that older consent screen. Older parent PIN and parental-consent storage keys are removed during app hydration. For that reason, this policy does not claim that the current build provides a functioning Parent Zone, PIN, analytics-consent switch or parent-controlled consent dashboard.

We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising. We do not knowingly ask children to provide their real-world contact details. Please contact us promptly if you believe a child has submitted personal information that should not have been provided.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Profile, progress and preferences: remain in local app storage until removed through available in-app controls, by clearing India Explorer's app data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. Exact in-app reset options may vary by release.
  • Postcard images: remain in the local postcard library until the postcard or app data is deleted. Temporary cache files created for native sharing are deleted by the sharing helper after the share operation when the platform permits.
  • Local diagnostic logs: are stored locally by the app until cleared by an available app control, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app. An exported diagnostic log can include device/browser details and the current page URL; review it before sharing.
  • Third-party services: information shared with another app, or processed by Google Fonts or a configured Firebase service, is subject to that provider's retention and deletion practices.

To request deletion of information that may have reached a service operated by us, contact us using the details in section 14. We may ask for limited information needed to locate the relevant record. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information about a child in your first message.

10. Security

The app stores player data in app-managed local storage and the reviewed Android manifest disables Android app backup. Temporary postcard files are written to the app's cache for sharing. Where Google services are configured and used, their processing is also subject to Google's security controls.

No app, device or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep the device and app updated, use an appropriate device lock, and supervise a child's use of sharing features.

11. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, a parent, guardian or individual may have rights to ask for access to, correction of or deletion of personal data, to ask how it is used, to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and to raise a grievance with the relevant authority.

Most player profile and learning information is stored locally, so you can manage it by using the app's available controls or clearing the app's data through Android settings. Contact us with questions about diagnostic information that may have been transmitted to a service operated by us.

12. International processing

When the app requests Google-hosted fonts, or if a Google service is configured and receives diagnostic information, the relevant provider may process connection or service data in countries where it or its service providers operate. Please review the linked provider privacy information for details about its processing and safeguards.

Quick answers for parents

Does my child need an account?
