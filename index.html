1Who we are

India Explorer is made by Nishaan Tech, an independent studio in India that builds learning apps for children. In this policy, "we" means Nishaan Tech and "the app" means India Explorer. The app is designed for children aged 3 to 10 and for use with a parent or guardian nearby.

The app has no accounts. Almost everything stays on the device and never reaches us.

3What we never collect

  • Your child's real name, address, phone number, email, school, photos, or voice recordings
  • Precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or files from the device
  • Advertising IDs. We show no ads and do not use advertising or marketing trackers
  • Anything for profiling, behavioural advertising, or sale to third parties

The app has no chat, no social features, no user-generated public content, no links to outside websites in the child area, and no in-app purchases.

4Analytics (parent-controlled)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to learn which lessons are confusing and where the app has problems. It is off by default and starts only after a parent or guardian turns it on. A parent can turn it off at any time in the Parent Zone. Consent is asked for again after one year.

What is sent when it is on

  • Quiz started, answered and completed (state name, number of questions, score), cards read, states completed, XP earned
  • Session and daily visit counts, streak length, days active, total XP, and the age band
  • Technical error type and a short error message, and how long saving progress took

What Google collects automatically

Firebase adds standard technical data: a random app-instance identifier (which changes if the app is reinstalled), device model, Android version, app version, language, and approximate country. According to Google, Analytics does not store IP addresses. We do not receive names or any information that identifies a child.

Event data is kept for no more than 14 months and is then deleted automatically.

Not a profile. We use this data in groups, to improve lessons. We never build a profile of an individual child and never use it for advertising.

5Permissions

  • Internet: used only for analytics when a parent has allowed it. Lessons work without it.
  • Audio settings: so sounds and read-aloud play at a comfortable volume.

The app does not ask for storage, camera, microphone, location, contacts or notifications permission. Read-aloud uses the text-to-speech engine already on the device, and nothing is recorded.

6Children and parents

We design India Explorer to follow children's privacy rules, including COPPA (United States), the GDPR rules for children, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and Google Play's Families Policy. In practice, that means:

  • Analytics is off until a parent explicitly agrees. It is never switched on by default.
  • The Parent Zone is protected by a PIN. Settings, data export, analytics controls, sharing and reset are for adults.
  • We do not track, monitor behaviour, or target advertising at children.
  • We collect only what the app needs to work.

If you believe a child's information was shared with us by mistake, contact us (section 14) and we will delete it.

7Postcards and sharing

Postcards a child creates are saved inside the app, in the child's Passport. They are not uploaded to us. A parent can choose to share a postcard through the Android share sheet, for example to a family chat. That sends the image to the app the parent picks, and that app's own privacy policy applies from then on.

8Third-party services

We use one outside service, and only after a parent opts in: Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC). Google acts as a processor for us. Its data may be processed on servers outside India. Read Google's Firebase privacy information and Google Privacy Policy.

The app has no ad networks, social login, or payment providers. Fonts, images and all lesson content are bundled in the app.

9Security

  • Data stays in the app's private storage and is excluded from Android backups.
  • The Parent PIN is stored in scrambled form, not as plain text.
  • Analytics data travels over encrypted connections.
  • Because we hold no accounts, there is no password database for anyone to steal.

No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to a minimum so there is little to lose.

10Keeping and deleting data

  • On the device: kept until you delete it. In the Parent Zone, choose Reset all progress to erase everything saved, or uninstall the app.
  • Export: the Parent Zone can export a copy of your child's progress.
  • Analytics: turn it off in the Parent Zone to stop new data. Data already sent expires after 14 months. Because analytics is not linked to a name, we may not be able to find one device's data in order to delete it earlier.

11Your rights

As a parent or guardian, you can ask to see, correct or delete information about your child, withdraw consent at any time, and complain about how we handle data. Most of this you can do yourself in the Parent Zone. For anything else, write to us (section 14). We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

12Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and its date, and ask parents for consent again before any new data collection starts. Earlier versions are available on request.

13Quick answers for parents

Does my child need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for, and no email or password.

Does the app show ads or sell data?

No. There are no ads and we never sell or rent information.

Does it need the internet?

No. Lessons, quizzes and postcards all work offline.

Can my child share things with strangers?

No. There is no chat or public sharing. Only a parent, in the Parent Zone, can send a postcard out.

How do I switch analytics off?

Open the Parent Zone, enter your PIN, and turn analytics off.
